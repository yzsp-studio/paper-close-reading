## 🇬🇧 English

Given one or a few academic papers, this skill performs a professional close reading: it explains the research question, the research story, the method or theoretical mechanism, the key evidence, the experimental results, the boundaries of credibility, reproducibility risks and research implications. It follows three principles: understand before evaluating, give the verdict before the details, and see the whole paper while deep-reading the key parts. It produces an advanced Markdown report and can generate a Feishu document. It is not for open-topic literature reviews, bare bibliographic verification, ghost-writing papers or language polishing.

### ✨ What it does

- Paper in → research question and research story pulled apart
- Explains the method or theoretical mechanism and the key evidence
- Checks experimental results and marks the boundaries of credibility
- Assesses reproducibility risk and research implications
- Outputs an advanced Markdown report and can generate a Feishu document
- States the verdict first, then unfolds the supporting detail
